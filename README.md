# Student Doctor Network (SDN) 

Welcome to the official GitHub organization for the **Student Doctor Network**. Since 1999, we have been dedicated to building a diverse doctor workforce by providing free resources, tools, and peer support to students who might otherwise lack access to professional advising.

## Our Mission

Our mission is to help students become doctors. We believe that health care is a fundamental right and that the path to a medical career should not be a for-profit venture. We provide a wide range of donor-supported resources to ensure that every student — regardless of financial resources — enters the application process on equal footing.

## Nonprofit Status

SDN is published by the **Health Professional Student Association (HPSA)**, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit educational organization based in California.

  * **Independent:** We have no corporate affiliations.
  * **Unbiased:** We maintain a strict firewall between our content and our sponsors.
  * **Volunteer-Driven:** Our community is built on the collective generosity of students, residents, and attending professionals.

## Privacy & Data Protection

We hold your privacy in the highest regard. Unlike many platforms in the educational space, we do not exploit student information for tracking or marketing.

  * **No Sale of Data:** We never sell or share member data with third parties for marketing purposes.
  * **Anonymity:** Our advising services and forums are designed to be used anonymously.
  * **Security:** We maintain a [Vulnerability Disclosure Program](https://www.studentdoctor.net/security-policy/) and welcome contributions from the developer community to help keep our platforms secure.

## The Future of SDN

The future of SDN is digital, decentralized, and data-driven. We are expanding our suite of free tools to include:

  * **AI-Powered Planning:** Tools like the Schools Database that adapt to student needs.
  * **Transparent Admissions:** Open-source trackers and calculators to demystify the cost and timeline of applications.
  * **Accessible Advising:** Developing "pay what you can" and 100% free on-demand advising modules to replace expensive private consulting.
